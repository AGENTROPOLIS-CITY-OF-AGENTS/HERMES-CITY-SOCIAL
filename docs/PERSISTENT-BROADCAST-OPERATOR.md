# Persistent Broadcast Operator Surface

HERMES-CITY-SOCIAL is an operator, coordination and external-platform adapter surface for persistent broadcasts.

It consumes:

- Social Systems persona, relationship and social-authority state
- ATG campaign and KOL/influencer activation plans
- ATV programs and channels
- Utility Grid `media.broadcast.*` leases and receipts
- BUZZ signed coordination events

## Operator controls

- inspect Persona Passport and active mandate
- review proposed segments and audience responses
- approve, reject, pause or redirect within policy
- view costs, provider health, moderation and fallback status
- take over or terminate the session
- preserve external-platform action receipts

HERMES does not turn social messages into authority and does not store provider credentials in model context.
